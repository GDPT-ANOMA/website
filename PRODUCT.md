# PRODUCT.md — GĐPT Anôma

## Register

**brand** — chapter site; design is the product of first impression for parents.

## Users & purpose

Primary: Vietnamese-American parents deciding whether to enroll kids, and current families checking Sunday schedule / events. Secondary: teens and huynh trưởng.

Single job: make a parent feel this is a serious, warm, well-run chương trình — and know how to show up on Sunday (time, place, what to wear, who to text).

## Brand personality

Grounded · Uniformed · Family-run

Not “zen wellness.” GĐPT Anôma: áo lam, sen trắng on three leaves, Bi–Trí–Dũng, ngành (Oanh Vũ / Thiếu / Thanh / Huynh Trưởng), Sen Trắng, hand-lettered Vietnamese, temple-hall Sundays.

## Anti-references

Generic Buddhist stock. Lucide/Material icon rows. Gradient blobs / glass / particles. Cream + terracotta editorial. Purple mesh. Three icon feature cards. “Join us on a journey / Empowering the next generation.” Cormorant + Public Sans AI default.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast. Visible focus. `prefers-reduced-motion`. Correct `lang` on VI vs EN passages. Mobile-first (parking-lot phone). Vietnamese diacritics must render cleanly.
